# HomeListingAI — 30-Day Launch Campaign: THE MARKET CHANGED. IT'S TIME YOU DID TOO.

(Chris's outline, saved 2026-10-13. Autopilot's "30-day launch" plan follows this file.)

## 1. The big idea
The mortgage market is brutal. Loan officers need a new way to compete.
Realtors are struggling. Some are leaving the industry. Existing referral relationships are producing less business.
Loan officers cannot afford to depend on yesterday's referral network. HomeListingAI helps them build tomorrow's.

## 2. Main message
YOUR REALTORS ARE QUITTING. YOUR REFERRALS ARE DISAPPEARING. WHAT'S YOUR PLAN?
You can't control mortgage rates. You can't control the housing market. You can't stop Realtors from leaving the industry.
**But you CAN control how you compete.**

## 3. Solution
HomeListingAI helps loan officers: find new Realtor partners, build valuable referral relationships, stay connected with potential partners, use AI to market themselves more effectively, create new business opportunities, build a stronger, more sustainable referral pipeline.
We're not selling AI technology. We're selling business growth.

## 4. Three advertising campaigns
- **#1 THE DISAPPEARING NETWORK** (fear of losing business): YOUR REALTORS ARE QUITTING. YOUR REFERRALS ARE DISAPPEARING. WHAT'S YOUR PLAN?
- **#2 YOUR COMPETITION ISN'T WAITING** (fear of falling behind): YOUR COMPETITION IS BUILDING NEW RELATIONSHIPS. ARE YOU?
- **#3 THE OPPORTUNITY** (confidence and growth): YOUR NEXT TOP REALTOR PARTNER DOESN'T KNOW YOU EXIST.

## 5. Channels
LinkedIn (daily posts), Facebook (bold ads, mortgage groups), Instagram (images and Reels), Email (five-part campaign), YouTube Shorts (15–30 s), TikTok, direct outreach, landing page.

## 6. 30-day plan
- **Week 1 — Wake them up:** Realtors leaving, referrals declining, a changing industry.
- **Week 2 — Turn up the pressure:** the cost of doing nothing; competitors building new relationships.
- **Week 3 — Introduce the solution:** how HomeListingAI helps loan officers identify and develop new Realtor partnerships.
- **Week 4 — Close the deal:** founding-member launch pricing, demos, signups. Use a deadline only if the offer really expires.

## 7. Founding LO launch offer
TOUGH MARKET. SMARTER TOOLS. SPECIAL LAUNCH PRICING. Early access, founding-member price, a chance to modernize the business. (Pricing and terms must be confirmed before publication.)

## 8. Visuals
Black backgrounds, fire-engine red headlines, tangerine-orange accents, large bold white type, cinematic images (struggling LOs, disappearing Realtor networks turning into growing AI-powered connections). The image should grab attention before anyone reads the copy.

## 9. Main call to action
STOP WAITING FOR THE MARKET TO SAVE YOU. START BUILDING YOUR NEXT REFERRAL NETWORK. [CLAIM YOUR FOUNDING LO LAUNCH PRICE]

## 10. Mission
1. The market has changed. 2. Doing nothing is a risk. 3. HomeListingAI offers a way forward: find new partners, build relationships, create opportunities.

**Final message:** THE MARKET CHANGED. IT'S TIME YOU DID TOO. BUILD YOUR NETWORK. BUILD YOUR PIPELINE. BUILD YOUR FUTURE.

**Golden rule:** every ad answers "How does HomeListingAI help me find my next producing Realtor partner?" Demonstrate it with what the product actually does, without promising guaranteed referrals or closings. The goal is signups and paying customers, not clicks or likes.

## Notes added by Claude (2026-10-13), so the posts stay true
- What the product really does today: gives a loan officer a **WOW Link**, a free co-branded listing page with an AI buyer chat that they send to an agent; the warm lead (name and phone) reaches the loan officer and the agent. That is the real way it "helps you find your next Realtor partner": it gives you something useful to start the conversation with. It does **not** search a database of Realtors for you, so Autopilot never says it finds or matches agents.
- No statistics about Realtors leaving. The posts say it plainly as a market view, with no numbers.
- Launch price facts: $49/month for the first 25 loan officers (regular $79), 7-day free trial, no card. No fake deadline: the offer ends when the 25 spots are gone.
