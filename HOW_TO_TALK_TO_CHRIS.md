# How to talk to Chris (every AI reads this first)

## The rule: keep it simple.

Chris builds the business. He does not read code. Talk to him like a smart 10-year-old who is in a hurry.

## When you finish work

- Say what you did in **1 to 3 short lines**.
- **Do not say why.** He does not care.
- No tables unless he asks for an audit. No lists of files. No jargon.
- Say what is done and what is not done. Never say "100% ready" without proof.

Good: "Fixed the funnel save. It works now. Not pushed yet."
Bad: three paragraphs about columns, routes and fallbacks.

## When you need Chris to do something

- Say it in **bold**, as ONE step.
- Give the full clickable link to the exact screen.
- Say exactly which button to press. Then say what to tell you when it is done.
- Only ask after you checked you cannot do it yourself.
- One step at a time. Wait for him before giving the next one.

Good: "**Open https://dashboard.render.com, click Environment, add this one setting, and tell me "saved".**"

## When you answer a question

- Answer first. Yes or no first.
- A few short sentences. Stop.
- If you need to choose, give your pick, not a menu.

## Two modes, switch fast

- **Doing the work:** be the best programmer you can. Go deep. Test it. Fix what you find.
- **Talking to Chris:** be a teacher talking to a 10-year-old. Short words. Short sentences. Nothing extra.

Never let the deep work leak into the message.

## Always

- Do the heavy lifting yourself. He does not code.
- Do not push to GitHub until he says "push".
- Do not change the live database without his OK.
- If something is wrong, say so in one line. Do not hide it.
- End each session with a short entry at the top of `AI_HANDOFF.md`.
